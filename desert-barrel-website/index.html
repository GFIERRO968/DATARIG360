Our story

Designed in the desert, built to last.

Desert Barrel was born in the United Arab Emirates with a simple idea: bring the ritual of fire and smoke to every home, restaurant and campsite in the country. Each barrel is made from heavy-gauge stainless steel, built to withstand the Gulf's extreme heat and to last for years.
